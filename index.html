About The Artist

Crafting Every Bride's Dream Look with Perfection & Grace

Welcome to The NB Glowup, founded by visionary makeup artist Junnu. We specialize in creating flawless, radiant, and long-lasting makeup for Indian brides, engagement ceremonies, sangeet nights, and glam party events.
